Detailed Description

A drawable surface in the NCurses backend. Wraps an NCurses WINDOW and its associated PANEL.

A drawable surface in the NotCurses backend.

Each UiSurface wraps a struct ncplane*. Visibility is simulated by moving the plane off-screen when hidden and restoring it when shown.
